# Hisaab: what changed in each version

This is the only page that tells the history. Every other page describes the version on the live link.

## Two versions

| Version | What it is | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1.5.2.1 | Today's version, on the live link | This branch, `main` |
| 1.0.0.0 | The first release, of 21 November 2026, with its documents as they were | Branch [`first-release-1.0.0.0`](../../tree/first-release-1.0.0.0) |

What today's version has that the first release did not:

| Area | Today |
| --- | --- |
| Shops | Several shops on one device, and a shop profile with owner, address and logo |
| Languages | Twelve instead of three, with Urdu right to left and names in each script |
| Customer page | Totals, call, WhatsApp chat, change phone number, delete with Undo, balance over time |
| Reminders | A tappable pay link, a WhatsApp check of the number, and other ways to send |
| Money | Whole rupees, with a one-tap nearest amount when paise are typed |
| Checks on what is typed | A date cannot be lost on an edit; a full or blocked browser store is said plainly |
| Layout | A chat-style layout on phone and desktop: quick entry bar, "+" menu, right panel |
| Look | The brand colours and fonts |
| Offline | Fonts and the QR library served from the site, so both work without a connection |
| Voice and quick entry | Voice always says why it failed; quick entry offers to add an unknown name |

The entries below are newest first.

## Version 1.5.2.1 (27 November 2026)

- The search box is easier to tap on a phone.
- The labels under the total wrap instead of being cut short.
- The "Record" label is whole in every language.
- A notice beside the reminder says when it carries the demo UPI ID, with a link to Shop settings, in twelve
  languages.

## Version 1.5.2.0 (27 November 2026)

- The desktop right panel has no scroll boxes: Overdue and Recent entries show in full.
- Each of those two lists has its own filter box.
- The brand mark in the top bar opens sai4b.com in a new tab.

## Version 1.5.1.2 (27 November 2026)

- On a phone the language box is an icon button beside the light and dark button.

## Version 1.5.1.1 (27 November 2026)

- The heading of the desktop right panel follows a change of language.

## Version 1.5.1.0 (27 November 2026)

- Urdu runs right to left; amounts and balances keep their own order.
- The desktop right panel fits the screen.

## Version 1.5.0.0 (27 November 2026)

- The chat-style layout reaches desktops: the quick entry bar sits at the bottom of the main column with its
  own "+" menu.
- On wide screens a right panel holds the full entry form, Overdue and Recent entries.

## Version 1.4.1.0 (27 November 2026)

- The "+" menu is in all twelve languages.
- Fonts and the QR library come from the site itself, so both work offline.

## Version 1.4.0.0 (27 November 2026)

- On a phone the ledger gets the screen, in a chat-style layout: a short top bar and a compact total.
- Quick entry is one bar at the bottom: type or speak "Sunita 500 udhaar" and send.
- A "+" menu holds every other action.
- Customers are on the first screen.

## Version 1.3.0.0 (27 November 2026)

- The brand look: navy, cream and amber, with Fraunces headings and Inter text.
- Money is in whole rupees. A typed amount with paise is held with a one-tap nearest whole amount.
- Imported amounts with paise are rounded and counted; stored ones can be rounded, with Undo.

## Version 1.2.6.0 (27 November 2026)

- A change the browser could not keep is said plainly, and "Saved." is not left standing.
- A notice when the browser is blocking storage, for example in a private window.
- Screenshots are kept at 600 px at most; an oversized imported picture is left out and counted.
- An edit cannot lose its date.
- The shop name shows whole on a phone.

## Versions 2.0.0.0 and 2.0.1.0 (26 to 27 November 2026, withdrawn)

- A rebuild of the app was on the live link for a day. It was taken off on 27 November and the full-featured
  line continued from 1.2.5.0.

## Version 1.2.5.0 (25 November 2026)

- The light and dark button says which theme is on.
- A press on a navigation item says which view is showing.
- The skip link puts the focus on the ledger.
- Quick entry with a name no customer has says so and offers to add that customer.

## Version 1.2.4.0 (23 November 2026)

- Voice quick entry never ends in silence: when the speech service hears nothing it says so.
- Each voice failure offers a way that works: the keyboard's microphone on a phone, dictation on a computer.

## Version 1.2.3.1 (23 November 2026)

- A wrong answer to the WhatsApp check is switched with one tap.

## Version 1.2.3.0 (23 November 2026)

- A WhatsApp check when a customer is added: a hello message to the number.
- The owner's answer, chat opened or refused, is remembered for each number.

## Version 1.2.2.0 (23 November 2026)

- Other ways to send a reminder when WhatsApp refuses a number: as an SMS, to a contact chosen in WhatsApp, or
  copied.
- A note on the sample shop's numbers, which are not real.

## Version 1.2.1.0 (23 November 2026)

- The layout holds in every language and at every width.
- Customer names are shown in the script of the nine newer languages.
- The WhatsApp reminder carries a pay link a customer can tap.

## Version 1.2.0.0 (23 November 2026)

- Nine more languages, twelve in all.
- Customer tools: totals, days since the last payment, call, WhatsApp chat, change phone number, delete with
  Undo.
- Note shortcuts under the note box.
- The total unpaid for 30 days or more, and the date the figures are for.

## Version 1.1.0.0 (22 November 2026)

- A shop profile: owner, address and logo, shown in the top bar and on statements.
- Balance over time on each customer page.
- Several shops on one device, with "Import as a new shop".

## Version 1.0.0.0 (21 November 2026)

The first release.

- Every required item of the specification: customers, credit and payment entries, balances and the total
  outstanding, payment screenshots, WhatsApp reminders, import of a shop file, and a 375 px phone layout.
- All seven optional features: overdue list, edit and delete with Undo, CSV export, search, a UPI pay link in
  the reminder, Hindi and Telugu, and the amount read from a payment screenshot.
- Quick entry by typing or by voice, a UPI QR code, "Remind all overdue", a statement, and offline use after
  one visit.
- The entry types read "You gave (credit)" and "You got (payment)", and the overdue badge starts at 30 days.
- On 22 November, with the number unchanged, voice quick entry began to say why it failed.
